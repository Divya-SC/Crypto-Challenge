Final Assessment

Attribution Conclusion

A confirmed real-world identity was not established through publicly available evidence. However, the combined evidence supports high-confidence attribution to a coordinated operational entity.

Execution Layer

Creator wallet, temporary accounts, Jito bundles, swaps, bridge activity, and execution-layer routing.

Campaign Layer

Metadata-linked website, X account, Telegram promotion, paid promotion, and repeated naming patterns.

Final Position: The CR7 token activity cannot be classified as organic market behavior. It reflects a coordinated token deployment model involving controlled wallets, bundled execution, cross-chain routing, metadata-linked campaign identity, and structured promotion.
Attribution Limitation: The investigation identifies a wallet cluster and campaign infrastructure, not a legally confirmed individual. Coinbase / exchange endpoints may hold KYC information, but that is not publicly accessible.
