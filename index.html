Final Assessment

13. Attribution Conclusion — Who Is Behind This?

A confirmed real-world legal identity was not established from publicly available blockchain and OSINT evidence. However, the investigation identified strong attribution indicators linking the CR7 token to a coordinated campaign infrastructure and a central operator wallet.

The creator / operator wallet:
8qNzt6DxDtZYj8jhrm8NP7sNsMz1QzudymKxS5GPCX4Q
was directly involved in token creation, transaction execution, swap activity, and fund routing.

Key Attribution Evidence:
  • On-chain metadata explicitly embeds:
    • https://x.com/CR7TheLastDance
    • https://cr7.life
  • The same identity is used across:
    • Token metadata
    • Website infrastructure
    • Social media promotion
  • Jito bundle execution confirms coordinated launch behavior
  • Wallet clustering and temporary accounts indicate structured control
  • Funds were routed through swaps, bridges, and ultimately into Coinbase-related wallets
Interpretation: The embedded metadata establishes that the CR7 token was intentionally linked to the @CR7TheLastDance identity at the time of creation. This strongly suggests that the same operator (or controlling entity) managed both the token deployment and its promotional infrastructure.
Important Limitation: While the campaign identity (@CR7TheLastDance) is directly linked to the token through metadata and promotional activity, publicly available evidence does not confirm the real-world individual behind the wallet.
Final Assessment: The CR7 token activity is consistent with a coordinated pump-style operation involving:
  • Centralized control of token deployment
  • Metadata-linked campaign identity
  • Structured promotion across X, Telegram, and web infrastructure
  • Multi-wallet execution using Jito bundling
  • Liquidity extraction and routing to centralized exchanges
The evidence strongly supports that the operator behind the creator wallet also controlled the CR7 campaign identity, forming a unified on-chain and off-chain operation.
Final Position: The CR7 token activity cannot be classified as organic market behavior. It reflects a coordinated token deployment model involving controlled wallets, bundled execution, cross-chain routing, metadata-linked campaign identity, influencer-style promotion, and structured exit toward centralized exchange infrastructure.
Most Likely Actor Type: A coordinated crypto trading / pump group experienced with Pump.fun launches, wallet distribution, social media amplification, and exchange off-ramping.
Attribution Limitation: EV / @HardSnipe and related channels are strongly linked to the promotional layer, but public evidence does not conclusively prove they owned the creator wallet. The strongest public attribution is therefore wallet-cluster and campaign-infrastructure based, not legal identity based.
