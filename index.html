CR7 Token Investigation Report

1. Case Overview

The CR7 token appears to be a coordinated meme coin launch leveraging the global popularity of Cristiano Ronaldo. The investigation identifies structured promotion, coordinated wallet activity, and strong narrative-driven marketing designed to attract retail investors.

2. Token Launch & Bundling Activity

The token launch was executed using Jito bundling, indicating coordinated multi-wallet execution.

3. Promotional Campaign Evidence

Multiple paid and coordinated promotions were observed across Twitter/X and influencer channels.
The marketing narrative heavily focused on Ronaldo’s “last World Cup” hype to create urgency and FOMO.

4. Social Media Manipulation

Frequent username changes and identity shifting indicate branding manipulation and potential obfuscation.

5. Telegram & Influencer Activity

Telegram promotion shows direct call-to-action messaging encouraging users to buy the token.

6. Website & Domain Evidence

The official website was recently created and uses privacy-protected WHOIS data.

7. Metadata & Token Structure

On-chain metadata confirms the token identity and links to IPFS-hosted content.

8. Fund Flow & Cross-Chain Activity

Funds were bridged and swapped across chains, indicating structured liquidity movement.

9. Insider Network Analysis

Wallet clustering shows interconnected accounts indicating coordinated participation.

10. Token Risk Indicators

High holder correlation and centralized ownership patterns indicate elevated risk.
While some tools show no insider networks, clustering evidence suggests otherwise.

13. Conclusion

The CR7 token demonstrates multiple high-risk indicators:
  • Coordinated launch using Jito bundling
  • Strong influencer-driven promotion
